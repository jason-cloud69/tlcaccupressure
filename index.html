About Your Therapist

Over a decade of hands-on care

I'm a licensed, certified massage therapist trained both in the United States and in Thailand, with more than 10 years in the field. Over that time I've worked across a range of styles and techniques — Swedish, deep tissue, and Thai massage among them — and I build each session around what your body needs that day, rather than a fixed routine.

If you're carrying everyday stress, chronic fatigue, or muscle aches that just won't ease up, that's exactly what I'm here for. You can expect a calm, respectful, and professional therapeutic session from start to finish.

Professional Massage Policy

TLC ACCUPRESSURE provides licensed, therapeutic massage only. This is a professional service — requests for sexual services, inappropriate comments, gestures, or behavior are not tolerated and will result in immediate termination of the session. Full payment remains due, and future appointments may be declined.

Clients are professionally draped throughout the session — only the area being worked on is uncovered at any time.
Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Repeated no-shows or late cancellations may require prepayment for future bookings.
